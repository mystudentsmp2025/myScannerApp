# MyScannerApp User Manual

Welcome to the **MyScannerApp**, a comprehensive boarding and deboarding solution designed for school bus transportation. 

This manual will guide you through the process of setting up a device, managing a bus trip, using various scanning methods, and viewing student activity history.

---

## 1. Initial Device Setup

When you open the application for the very first time on a new device, you will be prompted to authenticate your device and assign it to a specific bus. This ensures that all boarding logs generated by this device are accurately associated with the correct vehicle.

1. **Enter the School Code**: Type your school's unique branch code.
2. **Enter the Bus PIN**: Type the secure 6-digit PIN assigned to your specific bus.

*Note: Once authenticated, the app will securely save your `bus_id` and automatically load the daily route assignments for that specific bus.*

---

## 2. Managing Trips

Before you can begin scanning students on or off the bus, you must start an active trip.

### Starting a Trip
1. From the main **Dashboard**, locate the **"Start Trip"** button at the top of the screen.
2. Tap the button. The status icon will turn green, indicating that the trip is actively recording data. 
   *(Note: Starting a trip activates a continuous, high-accuracy GPS stream in the background to ensure scans are processed instantly without waiting for a satellite lock).*

*Important: If you forget to tap "Start Trip" and successfully scan a student, the app will instantly prompt you with a dialog asking if you'd like to automatically start the trip right then and there.*

### Ending a Trip
1. When your route is complete, tap the **"End Trip"** button at the top of the screen.
2. The status icon will turn gray. Any passengers still logged as being on the bus will be forcefully auto-deboarded.

*Smart Prompts: If you scan the final student off the bus (leaving the bus empty), the app will intelligently ask if you're ready to conclude the trip.*

---

## 3. Scanning Students

The app intelligently handles whether a student is "Boarding" or "Deboarding" based on their current status. If a student is currently off the bus, scanning them will securely log them as **Boarded**. If they are already on the bus, scanning them will log them as **Deboarded**.

### Double Scan Prevention (Already Scanned Warning)
To prevent accidental scans, the system includes a **60-second cooldown** per student. If a student mistakenly taps their card twice in rapid succession, the app will block the second scan and display a prominent yellow **"Already Scanned!"** warning. This ensures students aren't accidentally boarded and immediately deboarded within moments of each other.

*Bypass Note: This 60-second cooldown rule is intentionally bypassed if you manually tap "Deboard" from the manual roster list, or if the "End Trip" auto-deboard triggers, ensuring that intentional or systemic actions are never blocked.*

The app supports three different input methods for scanning:

### Method A: Camera Scanner (QR Codes & Barcodes)
1. Tap the **Camera** icon tab at the bottom of the screen.
2. Point your device's camera at the student's ID card or digital QR code.
3. The app will instantly register the scan, flash the screen green (Boarded) or red (Deboarded), and record the event.

### Method B: RFID Scanner
1. Tap the **RFID Reader** icon tab at the bottom of the screen.
2. Ensure your external RFID keyboard peripheral is attached. 
3. Instruct the student to tap their RFID fob or card against the reader.
4. The scan will process similarly to a camera scan.

*(For Emulators/Testing: Simply tap the grey "Ready to Scan" screen to open a manual testing dialog where you can type an RFID code like `MOCK-S-13` and simulate the hardware input)*.

### Method C: Manual Entry (Roster Search)
1. Tap the **Keyboard** icon tab at the bottom of the screen.
2. This displays a completely alphabetized list of every student assigned to your route.
3. Use the search bar to filter by a student's first or last name.
4. Tap the green **"Board"** button or red **"Deboard"** button next to a student's name to manually log their status if they forgot their ID card.

---

## 4. Recent Activity & Student History

### Recent Activity Feed
The bottom half of your dashboard always displays a live, scrolling feed of the most **Recent Activity**.
- Shows the student's photo, name, and exact scan time.
- A **Green Login Icon** indicates they boarded the bus.
- A **Red Logout Icon** indicates they deboarded the bus.

### Viewing a Student's History
If an operator needs to investigate a student's recent whereabouts:
1. Tap on any student's name inside the **Recent Activity** list.
2. A sleek **Student History Dialog** will pop up.
3. This dialog queries the live server and displays every scan event (both boarding and deboarding) for that specific student over the **past 48 hours**, including the exact time and the name of the bus stop where the scan occurred.

---

## 5. Reconfiguring the Device

If the device needs to be transferred to a different school bus:
1. Tap the **Gear (Settings) Icon** in the top right corner of the Dashboard.
2. Select **"Re-configure Device"**.
3. You will be returned to the **Initial Device Setup** screen, where you can input a new School Code and Bus PIN.
